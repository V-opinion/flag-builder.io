About the Flag Builder Project

The Flag Builder is an open, interactive web tool for creating, visualizing, and exploring Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, and Finnish national flags—including creative ccTLD variants (like .se, .no, .dk, .fi). This project empowers everyone to experiment with flag design for education, political inclusion, cultural campaigns, and multilingual communities.

Our mission is to foster democracy, visual literacy, and digital inclusion through open-source design, accessible on all devices and languages.  Flags matter: they are symbols of identity, pride, and sometimes challenge and transformation.

How to Use the Flag Builder Tool

  1. Select a Flag Template: Choose from Sweden, Norway, Denmark, or Finland. The original flag appears on the left; your editable version on the right.
  2. Add or Edit Elements: Drag existing shapes, add colored letters (like “.se”), or import other text and symbols. Tap/Click to move, change color, or resize.
  3. Compare & Export: See your creation next to the official flag; export as SVG, PNG, or share with others.
  4. Undo/Redo & Mobile Support: Make changes confidently. The interface is touch-friendly and performs smoothly across devices.

Flag Theory, National Identity & ccTLD Symbols

Flags are powerful tools of identity and communication, distilled into simple shapes and colors. National domains (ccTLDs, like .se for Sweden) are increasingly used on real-world flags for digital inclusion, branding, and grassroots movements.

Why remix flags? Adding ccTLD or multilingual symbols to a flag can represent open data, digital democracy, or highlight values shared across borders. Our Flag Builder enables anyone to visualize these changes transparently—no design skills needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I export my flag as an SVG or PNG?
Yes! Use the Export buttons for high-quality vector and raster images.
Is the app open-source and mobile-friendly?
Yes—Flag Builder works on all modern devices and browsers, and all code is open for contribution or learning.
What is a ccTLD?
Country Code Top Level Domains (ccTLD) are country-specific Internet extensions, e.g., .se for Sweden.
