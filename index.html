Learning the Alphabet Through Flags and Lego Pieces

  • Letter Placement: Drag or tap Lego alphabet blocks onto flags. Spell ccTLDs, words, or playful combos!
  • Color + Phonics: Each Lego block has a unique color for visual learning and fun.
  • National & Civic Literacy: Teach letter shapes, flag symbolism, and country codes together.

Teachers: Place the alphabet on flag templates, discuss colors and symbols, and help children build their own combination flags.

Why Place ccTLDs on Flags?

  1. Digital Identity: Recognize your nation’s presence online.
  2. Inclusion & Equity: Help developing countries claim their space in the digital era.
  3. Empowerment: Seeing your ccTLD encourages pride and participation online.
  4. Future Skills: Kids learn spelling and digital citizenship with each nation’s ccTLD.

Advocates: Use Flag Builder for digital inclusion and pride.
