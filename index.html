About the Flag Builder Project

The Flag Builder is an open, interactive tool for creating, visualizing, and exploring Swedish, Nordic, and religious national flags. It empowers everyone to experiment with design for education, inclusion, and democracy.

How to Use the Flag Builder Tool

  1. Select a Flag Template: Choose any country. The original appears left; your editable design on the right.
  2. Drag & Edit: Move, recolor, or resize crosses, crescents, stars, or add custom text or symbols.
  3. Export: Download your creation as SVG/PNG, or compare with the source side-by-side.
  4. Mobile Support: Works perfectly on phone, tablet, or desktop browser.

Flag Theory, National Identity & Religious Symbols

Flags make explicit both national and religious identity: crosses, crescents, and stars are present on many state flags. CC TLDs (like .se, .tr, .pk) can mark digital sovereignty and inclusion. Our Flag Builder helps test and visualize both historic and new symbolism.

Why remix? For digital citizenship, inclusion campaigns, open data, and civic innovation, remixing is a path to visual literacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I export my flag as SVG or PNG?
Yes, use the Export buttons for vector or raster images.
Can I move or recolor religious symbols?
Yes! All shapes are draggable and color-editable (try Turkey, Israel, Morocco, Pakistan, Vatican flags).
Is the app open-source and mobile-friendly?
Yes—Flag Builder works everywhere, and is open for contributions.
