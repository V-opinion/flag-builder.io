Fattiga länders toppdomän – Discover the Power of ccTLDs

Why ccTLDs? The top-level domain of poor countries is a key to education, inclusion, and democracy. Flags of nations should wave with their own ccTLD (.pk, .se, .ng, etc.) instead of borrowed religious symbols from EU, UN, or NATO. Replacing outside symbols with a country’s digital top domain honors local identity and makes flags more meaningful and respectful.

  • Education, inclusion, and democracy are strengthened by giving every country their own digital address.
  • Poor countries especially need visibility and empowerment in the digital world.

Modern Flag Design: The Real Conflict Resolution Model

The incomplete separation of church and state and religion and politics has not been able to resolve the 77-year-long conflict between Israel and Palestine. This is due to the unsustainable policies pursued primarily by religious parties, for example within the EU and other countries such as the USA, Israel, Lebanon, India, etc.

Unsustainable development leads to lasting war, lasting poverty, lasting occupation, and lasting settlements that lead to genocide.

Through a new modern flag design with educational purposes—where children in preschools can learn the alphabet from the flags together with the Multi-Religion Election System, which means introducing democracy in all religions without exception—a new modern conflict resolution model emerges. It solves the problem of the incomplete separation between state and church and between religion and politics. It is a guide to sustainable politics that leads to sustainable development.

  • Replace religious and difficult-to-interpret symbols with the country's own top-level domain, ccTLDs, to create a flag representing all citizens.
  • Children in kindergartens learn the alphabet from national flags—a peaceful flag for all.
  • Reminds poor countries of the importance of ccTLDs.
  • Creates many jobs in flag design and the IT sector—empowering refugees with meaningful work.
  • Example: Somali refugees in Sweden operate and promote the .so domain, fostering cooperation, communication, and integration.
  • Refugees from other groups and interested parties are welcome to join, learn, and participate in this model for effective conflict resolution.

This project: improves communication, language, cooperation, democracy and peace—serving as a scalable conflict resolution and integration model for the world.
