Learning the Alphabet With Lego Letters and Flags

  • Letter Placement: Drag or tap Lego alphabet blocks onto flags. Spell ccTLDs, words, combos!
  • Color + Phonics: Each Lego block has a unique color for visual learning and fun.
  • National & Civic Literacy: Teach digital domains, country symbols, and alphabet shapes together.

Teachers: Place the alphabet or ccTLDs on flag templates, discuss colors and flags, and help children build their own mix.

Why Place ccTLDs on Flags?

  1. Digital Identity: Recognize your nation’s address online (.se, .pk, .br, .us, ...).
  2. Inclusion & Equity: Help world countries find pride and equality through their digital domain.
  3. Empowerment: Seeing the ccTLD encourages pride and participation online.
  4. Future Skills: Kids learn spelling and digital citizenship with each nation’s ccTLD.

Advocates: Use Flag Builder for digital inclusion and citizenship!
