Learning the Alphabet With Lego Letters and Flags

  • Letter Placement: Drag or tap Lego alphabet blocks onto flags. Spell ccTLDs, country names, or fun words.
  • Color + Phonics: Each Lego block uses a unique color to support visual learning and letter identification.
  • National & Civic Literacy: Connect letter shapes to flag symbols; teach diversity, digital domains, and spelling together.
  • Audio Feedback: When you select a letter, hear its name spoken aloud in your chosen language.
  • Active Learning: Move and arrange letters for hands-on alphabet practice in class or at home.

Teachers, parents, and learners: Place the alphabet or ccTLDs on flag templates, discuss what each flag and letter means, and help children build creative, memorable combos!

Fattiga länders toppdomän – Discover the Power of ccTLDs

Why ccTLDs? The top-level domain of poor countries is a key to education, inclusion, and democracy. Flags of nations should wave with their own ccTLD (.pk, .se, .ng, etc.) instead of borrowed religious symbols from EU, UN, or NATO. Replacing outside symbols with a country’s digital top domain honors local identity and makes flags more meaningful and respectful.

  • Education, inclusion, and democracy are strengthened by giving every country their own digital address.
  • Poor countries especially need visibility and empowerment in the digital world.
